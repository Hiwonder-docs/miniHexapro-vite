1.1 Getting to Know miniHexa Pro ​

1.1.1 Introduction ​

miniHexa Pro is an intelligent hexapod robot powered by a high-performance ESP32 controller. The robot integrates dual-mode Bluetooth and Wi-Fi communication, supporting multi-terminal control through the app, PC software, and programming interfaces. The innovative 3DOF leg structure pairs with 18-channel PWM high-speed servos. Through built-in inverse kinematics gait planning and control algorithms, the system calculates 18DOF joint space trajectories in real time, delivering agile and versatile motion.

miniHexa Pro integrates an ultrasonic sensor, an IMU sensor, and sensor expansion interfaces. The robot can also operate with the WonderLLM AI Module. Powered by a high-performance ESP32-P4C5 dual-chip architecture, this module integrates a 2MP HD camera, a microphone, an HD display screen, a speaker, and a CI1302 audio chip. Deep integration of multimodal AI models spanning text, voice, and vision empowers the robot with voice recognition, QR code detection, and intelligent dialogue. These capabilities enable rich applications, including voice control, visual interaction, and intelligent obstacle avoidance. Comprehensive learning materials, development resources, and video tutorials facilitate an efficient development journey into hexapod robotics.

1.1.3 Precautions ​

Critical precautions regarding the operation and storage of miniHexa Pro:

  • Children under 12 years of age must not use this product due to small parts and sharp pins.
  • Minors must operate this product strictly under adult supervision and guidance.
  • This product contains small and sharp components. Do not swallow or press against them to avoid injury.
  • This product contains conductive parts. Never touch the electronic components with metallic objects when powered.
  • Do not force the robotic arm joints by hand after powering on to prevent severe servo damage.
  • Store the product in a cool, dry place when left unused for an extended period.
  • When the servos are powered and running, immediately stop operating if external resistance blocks the servo output shaft from rotating smoothly. Continuous operation under resistance leads to a stall condition where current surges dramatically, potentially burning out or permanently damaging the servo.
  • Servo damage caused by operational errors, including stalling, overloading, or incorrect mechanical installation, is not covered by the warranty. Always operate within rated specifications and prevent overload to maintain normal robot operation and equipment safety.

1.3 Battery Charging and Initial Startup ​

1.3.1 Charging Instructions ​

This section details the battery charging procedure. Lithium batteries cannot be fully charged prior to shipment due to transportation safety regulations. Charge the batteries for approximately 1 h before initial use.

Follow these steps to charge the batteries:

  1. Ensure the power switch is set to OFF.
  1. Insert two 18650 lithium batteries into the battery compartment, aligning them according to the positive and negative polarity markings on the battery holder.
  1. Replace the battery cover and fasten it using an M3 × 6 screw.
  1. Plug the charger into the DC charging port of the hexapod robot. The charger indicator glows red during charging and turns green once charging is complete.

1.3.2 Battery Safety Guidelines ​

  1. Always charge the batteries using the official 8.4 V charger provided in the kit.
  2. The charger status indicator glows red during charging and turns green when the battery reaches full charge. Disconnect the power promptly once fully charged to prevent overcharging.
  3. If the robot remains unused for an extended duration, charge the batteries fully, toggle the power switch on the expansion board to OFF, remove the batteries from the compartment, and store them in a cool, dry environment.

1.3.3 Operational Precautions ​

  1. Never force the servo joints by hand after powering on to prevent mechanical gear damage.
  2. Avoid keeping the servos at extreme boundary positions for extended periods to prevent motor stalls.
  3. Keep fingers away from the joint linkages of miniHexa Pro during operation to prevent pinch injuries.
  4. The servos of miniHexa Pro are precision components and wearable parts. High-intensity or extended operation may require periodic replacement.
  5. Connect to the robot exclusively through the companion Wonderbot app. Never attempt pairing directly through the Bluetooth settings menu of the phone using a PIN code.

1.3.4 Initial Startup ​

  1. Place all six legs of miniHexa Pro flat and parallel to the desktop in a standard prone posture before powering on. This configuration prevents potential servo damage caused by sudden torque application upon initialization.
  1. Slide the power switch to ON.
  1. miniHexa Pro executes an initialization routine and transitions to a standing posture, indicating a successful startup.

1.4 Interface Descriptions for miniHexa Pro ​

1.4.1 Control Board Overview ​

The control board of miniHexa Pro features a modular controller architecture that connects to the ESP32 core module via an onboard socket. The controller integrates an IMU sensor, 21-channel PWM servo ports, a buzzer, a custom button, I2C ports, and GPIO ports. This extensive peripheral support facilitates secondary development and functional expansion.

1.4.4 Core Module Overview ​

The development module is powered by an ESP32-WROOM chip. It features an onboard Type-C port for firmware flashing and serial communication. The left button is the IO0 button, which controls the electrical logic level of the corresponding pin to trigger custom firmware execution events. The right button is the EN reset button, which initiates a hardware system reset.
